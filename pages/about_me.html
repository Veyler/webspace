About Me

On this page, I'm going to give a general overview of myself, what I like, what I don't like, my passions, hobbies, stuff like that (Idk who I think I am, but whatever).

Who am I ?

Hi! I'm Veyler, a French 16yo student (As I said here). I started building websites two years ago, and I've picked up the basics over time. Still, I'm not very good at it (I tend to spread myself too thin across different languages and technologies, which doesn't help with learning). My goal for the future is to find a job in the IT field (not very specific, Ik, maybe in cybersecurity).

Why this website ?

As I said, I’m not very good at web development, mainly because I spread myself too thin across different languages and projects. But that’s not the main reason. Like many people who want to get into development today, especially those my age, I’ve fallen into the vicious cycle of artificial intelligence. Whenever I hit a roadblock or wanted to move faster, I’d turn to it. Pathetic, right? I really wanted to make progress and feel the satisfaction of seeing something and saying to myself, “I made that,” not some AI writing code I barely understood. So I cleaned out my GitHub of all my projects and decided to keep only the ones that were 100% my own work (you can see my projects here). This site was first and foremost a way for me to practice (especially CSS), although it serves other purposes as well, such as a portfolio.
